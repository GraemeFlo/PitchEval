📘 PitchEval — User Guide
A referee evaluation & match-logging tool for US Soccer Referee Coaches. Track a match live, then turn that record into a structured, coaching-focused evaluation of the crew. All data stays on your device.
Getting Started
1.Your Profile — On first launch, enter your name, US Soccer Coach ID, role, and grade to personalize reports. You can skip and set it later.
2.Match Details — On the Match tab, fill in teams, competition, officials, and format. Several fields auto-fill (below).
Match Details
DateAuto-fills with today's date. Editable.
KickoffAuto-fills with the real time when you first start the clock.
Full TimeAuto-fills with the real time when you select F/T.
WeatherAuto-fills with current conditions for your location.
FormatSet half length, extra time (and ET half length), and Kicks From the Mark — these drive the clock at H/T, F/T, and ET Half.
OfficialsReferee, AR1, AR2, 4th Official with their grades (Grassroots → FIFA).
Live Match Tracking
Clock — Press Start to kick off; it auto-syncs to 1st Half and keeps accurate time even if the screen locks or you switch apps.
Periods — H/T, 2nd H, F/T, ET, and Kicks From the Mark. Selecting H/T, F/T, and ET Half advances the clock to the correct running time based on your format.
Score & Fouls — The scoreboard updates automatically when a goal is logged, with a per-team foul tally beneath it.
Log an event — Tap a button (Foul, Offside, Yellow, Red, Goal, Penalty Kick) and fill in the tap-to-select fields.
Smart Sequences
Reckless foul→ Yellow Card form → CMI menu
Excessive force→ Red Card form
Yellow Card→ Foul form → CMI menu
Penalty Kick→ Foul form (→ CRE if the decision was incorrect)
Red Card→ CMI Red Card form
CMI & CRE
CMI (Critical Match Incidents) — Significant decisions to review in detail: Goal/No Goal, Penalty Decision, DOGSO, Red Card, Offside/Goal, Handling/Goal, Other, or N/A.
CRE (Critical Referee Errors) — Documented officiating errors: Wrong Card, Missed Red, Penalty Error, Offside Error, Wrong Restart, Positioning Error, and more.
Match Log
Every event is timestamped in the Match Log (bottom bar). Edit any entry (it re-opens the original form, pre-filled), Flag entries for post-match discussion, Delete, or Save the log as an editable text file via the iOS Share Sheet.
Ratings & Feedback
Ratings — Score each official against role-specific criteria on the US Soccer scale (7.0–9.0 in 0.1 steps). An Overall Average is calculated live per official. Record the capacity/receptiveness assessments and observations.
AI Feedback — Generates coaching-focused narrative for each official from the match record and ratings. Requires your own Anthropic API key (⚙️ settings); everything else works without it.
Finalize & Library
Finalize Match closes the log and shows a Match Overview — score, statistics, players with 3+ fouls, flagged incidents, and CMIs — where you confirm and finalize.
Finalized matches are saved to the Library tab, stored durably on the device so they survive app updates and storage cleanup.
Score Bands
<7.0
Below standard — significant errors, poor match control
7.0–7.9
Developing to competent — solid, with areas to improve
8.0–8.4
Good to very good — consistent, controlled performance
8.5–8.7
Excellent to superior — elite-level management
8.8+
Exceptional — world-class, benchmark performance
Data & Privacy
All match data, the library, and your profile are stored locally on your device. Weather uses your approximate (IP-based) location. AI feedback is sent directly to Anthropic with your own key — nothing routes through a third-party server.

How It Works
Five steps from kickoff to a finished evaluation
1
Set Up the Match On the Match tab, enter teams, competition, and officials — several fields auto-fill for you
2
Track It Live Start the clock and log fouls, cards, goals, and incidents as they happen
3
Rate the Officials Score each official against US Soccer criteria on the Ratings tab
4
Get AI Feedback Generate a coaching-focused report — edit it or translate it, then export or print
5
Save & Track Growth Save to your Library to follow each official's performance trend over time
You can replay this anytime from the Guide tab
