PitchEval Privacy Policy Effective Date: September 14, 2026 PitchEval (“PitchEval,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a referee evaluation and match-logging application designed for soccer referee coaches. We respect your privacy. PitchEval is designed so that your match evaluations and related information remain on your device by default. This Privacy Policy explains what information PitchEval handles, how it is stored, and when information may be transmitted to third-party services. 1. Information You Enter PitchEval allows you to enter information related to soccer matches and referee evaluations, including: * Match information such as teams, date, venue, competition, and match details * Referee and assistant referee names and certification information * Coach/evaluator name, role, grade, and coach identification information * Match events, including fouls, cards, goals, offsides, penalty kicks, and other incidents * Referee ratings and evaluation notes * Critical Match Incident and Critical Referee Error information * Match schedules and calendar information * Coaching feedback generated by the application * Photos, voice notes, and video clips that you choose to attach to an evaluation You decide what information to enter into PitchEval. 2. Information Stored on Your Device PitchEval stores your match records, evaluations, settings, and related information locally on your device. The application does not require you to create a PitchEval account or provide personal information to us in order to use the application. Photos, voice recordings, and video attachments are stored locally on the device. In the native iOS/iPadOS application, video files are stored in the application’s device storage. PitchEval does not upload your attachments to PitchEval servers. 3. iCloud Synchronization The native iOS/iPadOS version of PitchEval can use Apple’s iCloud services to synchronize certain match and evaluation information between your own devices when iCloud synchronization is enabled. iCloud synchronization is associated with the Apple account and iCloud configuration on your device. PitchEval does not operate the iCloud service or have independent access to your iCloud account. For privacy and storage reasons, media attachments such as photos, voice recordings, and videos are not included in PitchEval’s iCloud metadata synchronization. These attachments remain on the device on which they were created. Your use of iCloud is also subject to Apple’s privacy policies and terms. 4. AI-Assisted Coaching Feedback PitchEval includes an optional AI-assisted coaching feedback feature. If you choose to use this feature, information from your evaluation may be sent directly from the application to Anthropic’s API to generate coaching feedback. This may include information such as: * Referee evaluation ratings * Match events and incident descriptions * Evaluation notes * Information about the officials being evaluated * Other information necessary to generate the requested coaching feedback The AI feature is optional. You do not need to use it to record or review matches. PitchEval is designed to send AI requests using an API key supplied by the user. PitchEval does not operate an AI processing server of its own. Your use of Anthropic’s services is also subject to Anthropic’s applicable terms and privacy practices. Do not enter information into PitchEval’s AI feature that you are not authorized to disclose to an external AI service. 5. Weather Information PitchEval can automatically populate match weather information when that field is empty. To provide this feature, PitchEval may use an IP-based location service to obtain an approximate geographic location associated with your internet connection. PitchEval does not request or use your device’s precise GPS location for this feature. The approximate location is then used to request current weather information from a weather service. You may also enter weather information manually, and the weather feature is not required to use the rest of PitchEval. 6. Photos, Microphone, and Video PitchEval allows you to optionally attach photos, voice recordings, and videos to match events and evaluations. The application only accesses these types of media when you choose to use the corresponding feature and provide the applicable iOS/iPadOS permission. PitchEval does not continuously record audio or video. Voice recordings are created only when you activate the voice-recording feature. Video and photo content is selected or captured through the applicable device functionality. Media attachments are stored locally and are not uploaded to PitchEval-operated servers. If you use the AI-assisted coaching feature, media attachments are not automatically transmitted to the AI service merely because they are attached to a match. 7. Analytics and Advertising PitchEval does not intentionally use third-party advertising trackers or sell personal information for advertising purposes. PitchEval does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. 8. Data Sharing PitchEval does not operate a central user database containing your match evaluations. Information may leave your device only when necessary for an optional feature or service, including: 1. Anthropic — when you explicitly request AI-assisted coaching feedback. 2. Weather/location service — when PitchEval automatically obtains approximate location information to retrieve weather information. 3. Apple iCloud — when iCloud synchronization is enabled in the native application. PitchEval does not sell your information to third parties. 9. Data Retention and Deletion Because PitchEval stores most information locally, you control much of the retention of your information. You can delete match records, evaluations, schedules, and attachments through the application where those deletion functions are provided. Deleting the PitchEval application or its local application data may remove information stored only on that device. Information synchronized through Apple’s iCloud may remain subject to Apple’s iCloud storage and deletion mechanisms. Information sent to third-party services is subject to the applicable third party’s retention and deletion practices. 10. Security PitchEval is designed with a local-first approach. Match and evaluation information is stored on the user’s device rather than in a PitchEval-operated cloud database. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use the security features provided by your Apple device, including a passcode and available device encryption and authentication features. 11. Children’s Privacy PitchEval is designed primarily for use by soccer referee coaches and officials. PitchEval does not knowingly collect personal information from children as a separate user-registration process. Because users may enter information about individuals involved in soccer matches, including younger players or officials, users are responsible for ensuring that they have an appropriate legal basis and authorization to enter and use such information. 12. Third-Party Services PitchEval may interact with the following third-party services: * Apple iCloud, for optional synchronization in the native iOS/iPadOS application * Anthropic, for optional AI-assisted coaching feedback when requested by the user * IP-based location and weather services, for optional automatic weather information Those services have their own privacy policies and terms, which govern their handling of information received by them. 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to PitchEval or applicable requirements. When changes are made, the revised policy will be posted at this location and the effective date will be updated. 14.
