Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start a match.

Why does the Feedback tab ask for an API key?

The AI-written coaching narrative runs on your own Anthropic API key, which you enter under Settings. It is the only feature that needs one. Match tracking, incidents, ratings, the Library, and every export — including the printed report and the Development History — work without it.

Can I start a match on one device and finish it on another?

Yes, as long as both devices are signed into the same Apple account with iCloud enabled. Saved evaluations, your season schedule and the match in progress sync between them. Sync goes through your own iCloud, so it can take a few seconds to come across.

Why didn't my photos and video sync?

Attachments are deliberately not synced. Photos, voice notes and video clips stay on the device that captured them, so that large files never fill up your iCloud storage. Export or share them from that device.

I deleted something by mistake.

Deleting an event, incident or error shows an Undo option for a few seconds. Once that window passes the entry is gone, but deleting a saved match from the Library always asks you to confirm first.

Where is the weather coming from?

The weather field pre-fills using an approximate location derived from your internet connection, not your device's GPS. You can overwrite or clear it. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do I get my data out?

Every saved match can be exported as a printed or PDF report, as plain text, or as a full match log. The Development History export gathers one official's record across every match you have evaluated.

Reporting a problem

If something looks wrong in a match record, export the match log before doing anything else — it captures exactly what the app has stored and makes the problem much easier to track down. Attach it to your email.
