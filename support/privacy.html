# PitchEval Privacy Policy **Effective Date: September 14, 2026** PitchEval ("PitchEval," "we," "us," or "our") is a soccer referee evaluation and match-logging application designed for referee coaches and evaluators. We respect your privacy. PitchEval is designed with a local-first approach: most information you enter is stored on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information PitchEval handles, where it is stored, and when information may be transmitted to third-party services. ## 1. Information You Enter PitchEval allows you to enter information related to soccer matches and referee evaluations, including: - Match information such as teams, date, venue, competition, and match details - Referee and assistant referee names and certification or grade information - Evaluator or coach name, role, grade, and coach identification information - Match events, including fouls, cards, goals, offsides, penalty kicks, and other incidents - Referee ratings and evaluation notes - Critical Match Incidents (CMIs) and Critical Referee Errors (CREs) - Coaching feedback generated by the application - Photos, voice notes, and video clips that you choose to attach to an evaluation You decide what information to enter into PitchEval. ## 2. Local Storage PitchEval stores match records, evaluations, settings, and related information locally on your device. PitchEval does not require you to create a PitchEval account or provide an email address to use the application. Photos, voice recordings, and video attachments are stored locally on the device. PitchEval does not upload these media attachments to a PitchEval-operated server. PitchEval also stores an Anthropic API key locally when you choose to provide one for the optional AI features. The key is used to make requests directly from the app to Anthropic. ## 3. iCloud Synchronization The native iPhone/iPad version can use Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store to synchronize certain match and evaluation metadata between the user's own devices when iCloud is available. PitchEval does not operate or control Apple's iCloud service and does not have independent access to the user's iCloud account. Media attachments, including photos, voice recordings, and videos, are intentionally excluded from PitchEval's iCloud Key-Value synchronization and remain on the device where they were created. Your use of iCloud is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. ## 4. Optional AI-Assisted Coaching and Translation PitchEval includes optional AI-assisted coaching feedback and translation features. When you explicitly request AI feedback or translation, the application sends the information necessary to perform that request directly to Anthropic's API. Depending on the feature and the information in the evaluation, this may include: - Referee and assistant referee names - Match information - Performance ratings - Match events and incident descriptions - Evaluation notes and observations - Critical incident information - Other evaluation content needed to generate or translate the requested feedback PitchEval does not claim that this information is anonymous. Users should avoid entering information into the AI feature that they are not authorized to disclose to an external AI service. The AI feature is optional and is not required to record or review matches. PitchEval uses an API key supplied by the user for these requests. The API key is stored locally by the application and is transmitted to Anthropic as part of the direct API request. PitchEval does not operate an intermediary AI server. Anthropic's handling of information is governed by Anthropic's applicable terms and privacy practices. ## 5. Weather and Approximate Location PitchEval can automatically populate the weather field when it is empty. To do this, the application sends the device's public IP address to an IP-based geolocation service to obtain an approximate latitude and longitude. PitchEval does not request or use the device's precise GPS location and does not request iOS Location Services permission for this feature. The approximate coordinates are then sent to the Open-Meteo weather API to obtain current weather information. This weather feature is optional in the sense that the weather field can be entered manually, and the application can continue to be used if the weather request fails. ## 6. Camera, Photos, Microphone, and Video PitchEval may request access to the camera and microphone when you choose features that use them. The application may use the camera to capture photos for match events or incidents and may use the microphone to record voice notes. The application can also allow video attachments to be associated with match information. PitchEval does not continuously record audio or video. Media access occurs only when you activate or use the relevant feature. These media files are stored locally on the device and are not automatically sent to PitchEval servers. ## 7. Live Activities and Dynamic Island On supported iPhone devices, PitchEval can use Apple's Live Activities framework to display match information such as team names, score, period, and match-clock information on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. Live Activity information is generated locally by the app. PitchEval does not operate a push-notification server for the Live Activity feature. ## 8. PDF Export and Sharing PitchEval can generate PDF evaluation reports on your device. When you choose to export or share a report, iOS's standard sharing functionality is used. Any recipient or service you select through the share sheet receives the information you choose to share. PitchEval does not operate a server that receives these exported reports. ## 9. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking PitchEval does not intentionally include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising, or analytics services. PitchEval does not sell personal information. PitchEval does not use App Tracking Transparency to track users across other companies' apps or websites. ## 10. Third-Party Services PitchEval may communicate with the following third-party services: 1. **Anthropic** — when you explicitly request AI-assisted coaching feedback or translation. 2. **ipwho.is** — when automatic weather lookup is used, to obtain approximate location from the device's public IP address. 3. **Open-Meteo** — when automatic weather lookup is used, to obtain weather information for the approximate coordinates. 4. **Apple iCloud** — for optional synchronization of certain match and evaluation metadata in the native iPhone/iPad application. PitchEval does not sell your information to these services. Each third-party service may have its own privacy policy and terms governing information it receives. ## 11. Data Retention and Deletion PitchEval does not maintain a central database of user match evaluations. Because most PitchEval information is stored locally, you control the retention of that information on your device. You can delete records and attachments using the application's available deletion functions. Deleting the application or its local data may remove information stored only on that device. Information synchronized through iCloud may remain subject to Apple's iCloud storage and deletion mechanisms. Information transmitted to third-party services is subject to the applicable service's retention and deletion practices. ## 12. Security PitchEval uses a local-first architecture and does not maintain a central account database containing your evaluations. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use the security features available on your Apple device, including a device passcode and other available authentication and protection features. ## 13. Children's Privacy PitchEval is designed primarily for professional soccer referee coaching and evaluation. PitchEval does not knowingly create accounts for or directly collect information from children. Users may nevertheless enter information about participants in soccer matches. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have appropriate authorization to enter and use information about other individuals. ## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when PitchEval's functionality or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted at this location with an updated effective date. ## 15.
